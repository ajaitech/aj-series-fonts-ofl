The AJ Series (AJ Shresti, AJ Sahasvi, AJ Anushya, AJ Talin) is an original CAD-engineered enterprise typeface superfamily created by Aravind Jayamohan in India. Designed with zero sharp edges, exact uniform slim CAP-height numerals, WCAG AAA character disambiguation, 28 global currencies (including Unicode 17.0 Saudi Riyal and Unicode 18.0 UAE Dirham), and native Pan-European, Vietnamese, Arabic, Devanagari, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Japanese Kana, and Korean Hangul coverage.
