AJ Series & Aaahhh Fonts Vibe (v3.0.0) is a 5-family handcrafted multilingual enterprise typeface system (AJ Sahasvi, AJ Shresti, AJ Anushya, AJ Talin, and Aaahhh Fonts Vibe — 27 styles total) designed letter-by-letter by Aravind Jayamohan covering 14 world scripts, 28 currencies, extreme low-resolution OCR clarity, and Sound-of-Font phonetic shaping.
