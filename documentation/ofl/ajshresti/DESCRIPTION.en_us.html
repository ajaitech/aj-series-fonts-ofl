AJ Shresti is part of the original AJ Series 4-tier enterprise typeface superfamily designed by Aravind Jayamohan in India. Role: Level 2 · Sub-Headings (H2–H3) & System UI — Architectural Geometric Sans. Engineered with full Google Fonts Latin Core coverage, distinct per-family styling across 25+ native languages (Pan-European, Vietnamese, Arabic RTL, Devanagari Hindi/Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Japanese Kana) and 28 global currencies including Unicode 17.0 Saudi Riyal (U+20C1) and Unicode 18.0 UAE Dirham (U+20C3).

To contribute, see https://github.com/ajaitech/aj-series-fonts-ofl.
